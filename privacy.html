Threadroom privacy

Threadroom: A Dressmaker’s Tale is a dressmaking game from Odd Beacon Games. This policy explains the Android game’s data use. Last updated: 14 October 2026.

Your local atelier

The game saves your progress, maker name, designs, collection, options, client charges and purchase entitlement on your device. The advertising age-group choice is kept locally; we do not ask for your birthday. Local gameplay does not require an email address or a password.

Optional Google Play Games saves

If you choose Google Play Games sign-in and cloud backup, Google handles sign-in and stores a copy of your game progress using Saved Games. This can include the maker name and creations you save in your journal. The game uses your Play Games player identifier to associate the backup with your Google account. Odd Beacon Games does not receive your Google password. The Play Games SDK also processes gamer identity and service analytics and diagnostics. Google’s account and service settings control that account.

Optional rewarded advertising

The free Android version offers an optional advertisement to refill client charges. Client charges also refill with time, and market dressmaking remains available without charges. Premium removes the client-charge limit and rewarded-ad entry. The game does not insert advertisements into sewing.

Advertising uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform. Google’s advertising SDK can process IP addresses (which may indicate approximate location), device and advertising identifiers, app and ad interactions, and diagnostic information to deliver ads, measure performance and prevent fraud. Threadroom does not request precise device location. Where applicable, Google presents privacy choices before an ad can be requested. Advertising consent choices can be reopened in the membership privacy controls when Google requires that option.

Threadroom is intended for ages 13 and above. The game does not offer ads to players who select “Under 13.” Players selecting 13–17 receive under-age-of-consent advertising treatment. Age-appropriate ad settings are used, but an advertising preference is not proof of age.

Google Play purchases

Google Play handles payment for the optional, one-time Premium upgrade. The game receives the product identifier, transaction information and ownership state needed to fulfill and restore that purchase. Odd Beacon Games does not collect your payment-card number or billing details through the game. A purchase follows Google Play’s checkout, refund and account rules.

The optional community salon

The online community salon is not enabled in the initial Android release candidate. When enabled, sharing is optional and separate from local play. A maker profile, maker name, chosen creation title, rendered dress image, likes, reports and blocking information are processed by the salon backend hosted on Supabase. Shared names and approved creations are visible to other salon users. A salon image is rendered from the game’s dress; the game does not request access to your camera or personal photo library for this feature. Please avoid putting real names, contact details or other personal information into public maker names or titles. Community access is for ages 13 and above.

Support and security

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any information you choose to include. We use it to answer the request, investigate problems or process a deletion request. Do not send passwords, payment-card details or account sign-in codes. Network services use encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Retention and your choices

Local data remains on the device until you remove it using the game’s options or Android’s app-data controls. Uninstalling may remove local data; an optional Google cloud backup can remain associated with your Google account. Removing local progress does not cancel a Google Play purchase or erase a separate cloud backup.

Community data, when that feature is enabled, is retained to operate the salon until removed or a deletion request is completed. Security, moderation and transaction records may need to be retained for fraud prevention, disputes or legal obligations. Service providers apply their own retention practices to data they process. We do not promise that clearing the game removes data held by Google or other independent services.

See data deletion and account help for local, cloud and community deletion options. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct or delete personal data. Contact us to make a request.
