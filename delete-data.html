Threadroom data deletion

For Threadroom: A Dressmaker’s Tale by Odd Beacon Games.

Request deletion or account help

Email oddbeacongames@gmail.com with the subject “Threadroom data deletion.” Say whether you want help with a local save, a cloud backup, or a community maker profile. If the community salon is enabled and you use it, include your maker name and the maker tag shown in your profile. We may need to verify ownership before removing an account. Never send your password, authentication codes or payment-card details.

The community salon is disabled in the initial Android release candidate. No community profile is created by local play alone.

Local progress

Android Settings → Apps → Threadroom → Storage → Clear data removes this installation’s local game data and preferences. Back up a journal you wish to keep before doing this. Local deletion does not delete a Google cloud backup or a Play purchase; signing in or restoring a backup can bring progress back.

Google Play Games backup

Google manages Saved Games data. In the Google Play Store app, open your profile → Settings → General → Play Games Profile → Privacy & Settings → Delete profile & game data. Under “Delete individual game data,” choose Threadroom. You can also visit your Play Games profile in a browser, open Your Data → Delete Play Games Account & Data, and find Threadroom under individual game data. Check Google’s current instructions before confirming; deleting game data can be permanent. Removing a game’s Play Games data does not delete your Google account.

Purchases and retained records

Google Play retains its purchase and billing records. Clearing Threadroom does not refund a purchase. For a Google Play refund, use Google Play’s help and purchase history. We may retain limited support, transaction, security or moderation records when required for disputes, fraud prevention or legal obligations.
